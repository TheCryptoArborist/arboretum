How do I claim my season rewards?

Open Rewards → Monthly Pool → Claim Reward. Choose an eligible Seed, review the wallet request, and confirm it. Repeat for any other eligible Seeds. Your rewards are not sent automatically. Claim availability depends on your Seed, the season, any pause, and the applicable archive deadline.

A projected amount is not proof of payment. Check the confirmed reward after the transaction completes.
