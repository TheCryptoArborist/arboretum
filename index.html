Daily Strategy
Every useful move should create, protect, or multiply GP. Watering keeps Seeds alive, but strategy is how you climb. Check your watering window, protect risky Seeds, use Arborist Items before high-value moments, and add more active Seeds when you want more paths to earn Growth Points.
Core Rule
Final Growth Points decide payout share. When the 30-day cycle ends, your share is based on your Growth Points compared with total player Growth Points. More active Seeds, longer streaks, smarter item timing, and survival all improve your final position.
1Show up for 30 daysConsistency is the base strategy. Water ready Seeds throughout the full cycle so you keep adding Growth Points while inactive players fall behind.Daily habit creates the score.
2Keep every GP path aliveEach live Seed is another way to earn. Use protection items before missed time turns into lost Growth Points.Protect the paths that pay.
3Use crate items for edgeCrates create Arborist Items. Timed well, those items boost waterings, build streaks, recover Seeds, and help you pass players who only water.Items are the competitive edge.
4Push before the cycle endsWatch your pool share near the end of the 30 days. If another player is close, add Seeds, open crates, or use items before final GP is locked.Final GP decides the pool share.
End-of-cycle mindsetThe number that matters most is your final Growth Point total when the 30-day cycle closes. Daily choices are small, but they compound into final pool share.
Side contests are separateDaily Canopy Challenges are planned as optional side contests. The main live game is still the monthly Growth Pool, and that payout is decided by Growth Points.

Pick the level of involvement that fits you. You can change your approach any time during the monthly cycle.

Casual Survive and stay consistent Water daily, keep one Protection Item ready, and open smaller crates when you need basic support. This path is about keeping your GP paths alive with low effort.
Balanced Grow multiple Seeds Fill more slots, rotate Arborist Items across Seeds, and save Recovery or Protection Items for emergencies. This gives you more chances to build Growth Points without relying on one Seed.
Competitive Push leaderboard pressure Use higher-tier crates for stronger Arborist Items, time boosts around watering, protect your best Seeds, and keep watching your GP share as other players enter.

Arborist Items fall into five roles. Read the role first, then the item name. The role tells you why you would use it.

💫Recovery ItemsBring a dead current-cycle Seed back into play.
💎Power EnhancersInvest in your best Seed for a stronger rest-of-cycle push.
Crates versus one-off itemsCrates give variety and upside. One-off items are best when you need a specific fix, like a revive or protection item.
Referrals support strategyForest Invites can earn instant SUI when new players plant, giving active promoters another way to build value while still competing in the pool.
$TREE utilityPlanned $TREE spends should focus first on cosmetics, season-pass style unlocks, and convenience perks. This gives holders something to do without turning the Growth Point race into pure pay-to-win.

Harvest Math
Keep Seeds alive, grow more GP than other players, then claim your share of the monthly SUI Growth Pool.
Payout = floor(player_GP x pool / total_GP).
GP per water = 10 + min(streak x 2, 50). Fertilizer adds 50%. Miracle Grow doubles the next watering.
Paid actions are designed to split 70% to the reward pool, 28% to the project treasury, and 2% to the developer wallet.
When a direct invite reward applies, it is paid first; the remaining amount follows the 70 / 28 / 2 split.
Daily Canopy Challenges are optional player-funded contests. They are separate from the monthly Growth Pool and should only pay out what players choose to enter.
Claiming unlocks after the monthly cycle ends and happens one Seed at a time.

    Plant Seeds: one held NFTree NFT plants one Seed + 0.01 SUI. Referral and fee splits are shown from live contract data where available; future economy changes need contract support before they are treated as active. Water daily through the monthly cycle.
