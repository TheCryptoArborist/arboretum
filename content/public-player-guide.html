01 / GET STARTED

What do I need to buy first?

An NFTree, a planted Seed and a crate are three different things. Here is the order.

FIRST / NFTREE ACCESS

Choose an eligible NFTree.

Your NFTree is the access NFT. Each eligible NFTree supports one active Seed slot, up to eight slots per garden. Keep the associated NFTree in the wallet you use to play.

Make the separate planting payment.

02 / THE DAILY LOOP

A few decisions. Every visit.

Keep the garden active without buying something every day.

CHECK

See what each Seed needs.

Open My Garden and review the status, next-watering countdown and active effects on each card. Seeds have individual timers, so some may be ready before others.

PREPARE

Apply the right tool first.

A watering boost needs to be applied before the watering you want it to improve. Instant-point tools work differently. Check the selected Seed and read the tool’s effect.

WATER

Water when ready.

Use Water for one Seed or Water Ready Seeds for eligible Seeds. Review the wallet request, approve the action and wait for confirmation before repeating it.

REVIEW

Watch your progress.

Check Growth Points, watering streaks and remaining tool uses. Visit Rewards to see the season’s pool and your changing projected share.

How do timing and streaks work?

The current ordinary watering design uses an interval of about 21 hours 36 minutes, rather than a midnight reset. Use the readiness countdown for the Seed you are watering; timer-changing tools can affect its schedule.

Consistent watering builds a streak. Missing days can reset that streak, and leaving an unprotected Seed too long can lead to wilting and death. A dead Seed can lose its accumulated Growth Points; revival does not restore points that were removed.

Protection is for the selected Seed, not automatically the whole garden. Check its expiry and do not treat a reminder or a protection item as a promise of automatic point earning.

Are growth stages the same as rewards?

No. Growth stages show your Seed’s progress. They are not the same as its age, a leaderboard rank or a claim deadline. Reaching Season Harvest does not allow you to claim before the season closes.

04 / PLAY WITH A PLAN

Timing makes a difference.

More boosted waterings, not a bigger percentage.

Fertilizer adds three +50% waterings; Watering Boost adds one. Together they prepare four boosted waterings, not one +100% watering.

Check for a doubling already queued.

Miracle Grow, Double Dose and Crystal Water share a next-watering doubling effect. Do not assume applying another gives you two separate doubled waterings.

Match the tool to the season.

A tool that supports many future waterings is most useful when you have time left to use them. An instant-point item has a different role from a longer-running boost.

Protect or revive?

Protection helps before a Seed dies. A Revival Kit is for recovery. A growth tool is not a substitute for a revival action on a dead Seed.

05 / KNOW WHAT YOU ARE OPENING

Choose your crate by its role.

Buy a crate, open it to receive tools, then choose which tools to apply. A higher tier changes the tool mix—it does not guarantee a larger SUI payout.

What is a Supply Drop?

A Supply Drop offers a featured tool directly rather than a mixed crate. Check the featured item, its uses and its current price. Buying the item does not automatically apply it to a Seed.

The launch shop will show the final price, guaranteed contents, use counts and any bonus odds before purchase. This handbook does not present temporary prices or provisional drop rates as final launch terms.

07 / THE SEASONAL COMPETITION

How the SUI Growth Pool works.

The Growth Pool is the funded SUI pool players compete to share. Eligible gameplay payments contribute to it under the season’s allocation rules. Your eligible Growth Points are compared with the total eligible points of all players.

Your eligible Growth Points ÷ Total eligible Growth Points = Your pool share
Your pool share × Funded distributable SUI pool = Reward, subject to the season’s rules.
Show me a simple example.

With 100 eligible points out of 1,000 total eligible points, your share would be 10%. If the season’s distributable pool were 50 SUI, that share would be 5 SUI before any applicable rounding.

Those are illustrative numbers, not a live balance or an earnings forecast. During a season, points and the funded pool can both change.

When do I claim?

Claims follow the completed season’s rules. Check the Rewards screen for the close time, eligibility and claim deadline. A projected amount during play is not a finalized entitlement.

The public-launch claim window and final settlement rules will be published before participation opens. This guide does not promise an unlimited claim window or an automatic payout.

Rewards are not guaranteed to cover the cost of an NFTree, planting, crates, tools or network fees.

09 / QUICK ANSWERS

Before you enter the garden.

Can I read the guide without connecting a wallet?

Yes. This handbook is public. No wallet, NFT purchase or access code is required to read it. Gameplay remains restricted until public opening.

Do I have to buy something every day?

No. Daily care is about reviewing your Seeds, watering when ready and making use of the tools you choose to hold. Crates are optional; wallet actions can still incur network fees.

Can I use TREE for purchases?

The documented gameplay purchase path uses SUI. TREE checkout is not an available payment option described by this handbook. Do not send TREE manually expecting a Seed, crate or tool.

What happens if I sell or transfer my NFTree?

Keep the associated NFTree in the wallet you use for its Seed. Losing that access can prevent garden actions. Do not assume transferring an NFT also transfers a Seed’s points or a reward entitlement.

What if a purchase does not appear immediately?

Wait for confirmation and refresh your inventory before purchasing again. Check the connected wallet and current season. A delayed screen update is not a reason to approve the same purchase twice.

What is still being finalized?

Public opening, final prices, crate odds, tool balancing and application limits, and season-end claim terms will be announced before public play. Rarity bonuses are labeled as planned. Automatic watering for Bottomless Can is not a confirmed launch feature.
