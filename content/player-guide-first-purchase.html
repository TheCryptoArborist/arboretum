Before buying a crate

What do I need to buy first?

Your first game purchase is an eligible NFTree—not a crate.

The NFTree provides planting access. Next, pay the separate planting fee to create your Seed for the current cycle. Crates and tools are optional extras; you do not need to buy them to plant and begin watering.

Already own an eligible NFTree? Use it from the same wallet you connect to Arboretum. You do not need to buy another NFTree just to start, provided the one you hold is available for planting in the current cycle.

Before checkout: have SUI in that wallet for the purchases you choose and network fees. Keep some SUI available for later game transactions rather than spending the entire balance on the first purchase.

FIRST / REQUIRED ACCESS

Obtain an NFTree

If you do not already hold an eligible NFTree, use the game's Mint NFTree option and review its checkout before confirming. The NFTree is your access NFT; it is not a planted Seed or a crate of tools.

Start with one. You do not need to fill all eight Garden slots before playing.

SECOND / SEPARATE PLANTING PAYMENT

Plant your first Seed

Open My Garden, select an available NFTree and approve the planting payment shown by the game. This creates the current-cycle Seed that you will water and grow.

The NFTree purchase does not include this separate planting fee. Simply owning an NFTree does not automatically plant it.

THIRD / BEGIN PLAYING

Check your Seed and water when ready

Wait for the planting transaction to confirm, then check My Garden. Review the Seed's watering status and return when it is ready. An additional crate purchase is not required for ordinary watering; network fees still apply.

Keep the attached NFTree in your wallet while using that Seed's game access.

LATER / OPTIONAL PURCHASES

Choose crates or tools when useful

After learning the basic loop, compare standard crates, BOOM/Victory chests and Supply Drops. A bought crate must be opened to reveal its tools; those tools must then be applied separately.

Seedling Crate is a tool bundle—not the Seed you plant. No crate replaces the NFTree or the planting payment.

What should I budget for before I start?

New player without an NFTree: the NFTree checkout amount + one Seed planting fee + network fees. Your starting crate budget can be zero.

Existing holder with an eligible, available NFTree: one Seed planting fee + network fees. You do not need another NFTree purchase for that first Seed.

Check the current checkout amounts rather than adding together old screenshots or proposed launch prices. Testing amounts are temporary, and this guide does not approve production prices.

TREE tokens, NFTrees, Seeds and Seedling Crates are different.

Holding TREE tokens alone does not provide NFTree planting access in this reviewed build. TREE checkout remains a proposal; do not send TREE manually expecting an NFTree, a Seed or a crate.

Basis: the reviewed wallet's separate NFTree reference and SUI planting payment, and the existing guide's NFTree-access model. View planting transaction construction. Live eligibility and checkout remain authoritative while this guide is under tester review.
